# Starting points for the literature review
Read and verify each one yourself before citing it; only cite what you've actually used.

- Zervas, G., Proserpio, D., & Byers, J. W. (2021). A first look at online reputation on Airbnb, where every stay is above average. *Marketing Letters, 32*, 1-16. (Why ratings pile up near 5, which justifies a binary cut-off.)
- Gunter, U. (2018). What makes an Airbnb host a superhost? Empirical evidence from San Francisco and the Bay Area. *Tourism Management, 66*, 26-37.
- Ert, E., Fleischer, A., & Magen, N. (2016). Trust and reputation in the sharing economy: The role of personal photos in Airbnb. *Tourism Management, 55*, 62-73.
- Wachsmuth, D., & Weisler, A. (2018). Airbnb and the rent gap: Gentrification through the sharing economy. *Environment and Planning A, 50*(6), 1147-1170. (Canadian context: commercial multi-listing hosts.)
- Hosmer, D. W., Lemeshow, S., & Sturdivant, R. X. (2013). *Applied Logistic Regression* (3rd ed.). Wiley. (Methods: LRTs, calibration, diagnostics.)
- Inside Airbnb (2026). Dataset citation; see README.
